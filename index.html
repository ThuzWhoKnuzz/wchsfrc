About us

Engineering the next generation of problem solvers.

West Campus FRC is a student-run FIRST Robotics Competition team from West Campus High School in Sacramento, CA. We design, build, and drive a competition robot each season, and we treat every build day as a chance to learn something new.

Our team is built on collaboration, curiosity, and ambition. Members take on real engineering challenges, grow into leaders, and work as one team on the field.
